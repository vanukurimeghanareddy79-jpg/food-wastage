📋 How It Works

Three simple steps to rescue surplus food and support your community.

🍽️

1. Donate Surplus Food

Restaurants, households, events, and grocery stores submit safe, surplus food through the donation form with pickup details and safety information.

🔍

2. NGOs & Volunteers Find Food

Community kitchens, shelters, and volunteers browse available donations, filter by category and location, and request a pickup.

🤝

3. Complete the Pickup

Once food is collected and distributed, the donation is marked as completed, updating the community impact dashboard.

🌍

4. Reduce Food Waste

Every rescued meal means less organic waste in landfills, lower greenhouse gas emissions, and more food reaching those who need it.

🛡️ Food Safety & Responsible Donation

Food safety is everyone's responsibility. Please read these guidelines before donating or collecting food.

✅

Only Donate Safe Food

Donate food that is clean, properly stored, and genuinely suitable for consumption. Do not donate food that is expired, spoiled, contaminated, or has been improperly handled or stored.

🌡️

Correct Storage & Temperature

Perishable foods such as cooked meals, dairy, and meat must be kept at safe temperatures during storage and transport. Hot food should stay hot; cold food should stay cold.

⏰

Expiry Is Only One Factor

Expiry or best-before dates alone do not determine food safety. Storage conditions, packaging integrity, handling, and appearance all matter. Use good judgement and follow applicable food safety guidance.

⚠️

Declare Allergens

Always declare known allergens such as nuts, dairy, gluten, and eggs. Recipients may have serious food allergies. Provide ingredient information where available.

📦

Proper Packaging

Pack food in sealed, clean containers. Avoid loose or open packaging for cooked meals and fresh produce. Label containers clearly with contents and date.

🏛️

Follow Local Regulations

Food donation laws and requirements vary by location. This application does not verify compliance with local regulations. Donors and recipients are responsible for following applicable laws.

⚠️ Important Disclaimer: This application does not independently verify food quality, safety, or suitability. Submitting a donation through this platform does not certify that the food is safe to eat. All parties must exercise their own judgement and follow food safety best practices. ℹ️ About Smart Food Rescue Network

A college hackathon project demonstrating how technology can connect surplus food with communities in need, reduce food waste, and promote environmental sustainability.

🌿

Mission

Prevent edible food from going to waste by connecting donors with NGOs, volunteers, and community kitchens.

💡

Technology

Built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. Runs entirely in your browser. No server or internet required.

🔒

Privacy

All data stays in your browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to external servers.

🚀

Future Vision

A real-time shared network with backend integration, live maps, NGO verification, and push notifications.
